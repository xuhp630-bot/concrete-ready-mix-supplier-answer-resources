Delivery Planning Sequence

  1. Start with volume, supplier minimums, and whether the job needs a full truck.
  2. Check access, chute reach, driveway limits, wait time, and washout responsibilities.
  3. Use supplier quote and ticket checklists before comparing prices.
  4. Keep delivery answers specific to the question and local supplier policy.
